## 👋 About Me

I'm **Noory**, an AI developer based in Faisalabad, Pakistan. I'm studying **BS Artificial Intelligence at Riphah International University**, and I build machine learning, deep learning and computer vision projects in Python.

Alongside AI, I work in **social media management and content creation**. I use AI tools to plan content strategies for Instagram and TikTok, and I design visuals in Canva. I enjoy projects where AI solves a real problem and where good content helps people understand it.
